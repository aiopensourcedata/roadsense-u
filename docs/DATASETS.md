# Dataset access and suitability

Review date: 2026-10-24. Official pages and access notices were inspected; restricted video files were not downloaded. Availability of a page is not proof that all files can be fetched or that their terms permit a specific reuse. No bulk dataset downloader is provided until access, exact file layouts, licensing and disk budgets are resolved.

| Dataset | Access / terms checked | Annotation compatibility and limitations |
|---|---|---|
| **Nexar Collision Prediction, 2025** | [Official dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/nexar-ai/nexar_collision_prediction/blob/main/README.md) requires a gated access request, organization/contact information and acceptance of the Nexar Open Data License. Do not rely on older MIT announcements or third-party mirrors. | 1,500 dashcam clips; binary collision/near-collision target, event/alert times and environment metadata. Test truncation horizons differ. Label names include `time_of_event`, `time_of_alert`, `time_to_accident`. Boxes/pair identities and reliable fixed-camera location grouping cannot be assumed. Ego-motion invalidates the prototype's static homography without compensation. |
| **TU-DAT, 2025** | [Official repository](https://github.com/pavana27/TU-DAT) links a Google Drive folder and `annotations.zip`; README says annotations were based on YOLOv8. Public hosting does not resolve original footage redistribution rights. | Real and simulated roadside anomaly footage. Paper describes boxes and temporal/context annotations; archive layout and manual validation quality were not inspected, so no parser/download compatibility is asserted. Resolve event timestamps, clip identities and label quality on an authorized small sample first. |
| **TUMTraf-V2X, CVPR 2024** | [Official dataset site](https://tum-traffic-dataset.github.io/tumtraf-v2x/) declares CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and links dataset distribution and a dev kit. | **BrnoCompSpeed** | [Maintainer repository](https://github.com/JakubSochor/BrnoCompSpeed) directs users to contact the maintainer; warns about roughly 200GB. | **DoTA, 2020/2022** | [Official repository](https://github.com/MoonBlvd/Detection-of-Traffic-Anomaly) publishes download links and code under MIT. Verify video terms and source-footage permissions separately; code license alone does not settle dataset reuse rights. | Temporal/spatial/categorical driving anomalies and object locations. Anomaly detection labels need conversion to a causal future-event target and camera-motion handling. Downloaded-file structure and geographic IDs have not been verified. |

## Local fixtures actually used

`generate_synthetic_data.py` creates a 100-frame AVI of two colored objects crossing, known image trajectories, an illustrative synthetic homography and a deterministic tabular benchmark at eight fictional locations. Files stay under ignored `data/`. Colored detections, known points and labels are **synthetic measurements**, not real-world validation or estimates of road crash risk.

A second helper creates a repeated-frame video from the official Ultralytics bus image to exercise real pretrained detection and ByteTrack. It is a static-image smoke fixture, not real moving traffic. Source URL and SHA256 are recorded; upstream image/model license terms apply. Only small explicit model/image downloads are performed, not traffic datasets.

## Data acquisition checklist before future adapters

Obtain access and read current terms; record exact version/source and attribution; inspect a permitted small sample plus annotations; validate timing, coordinate systems, consent and label semantics; estimate compressed/extracted disk needs and reserve capacity; check camera identities and duplicate sources; then implement a versioned adapter with checksums, bounded downloads and explicit user-triggered acquisition. Do not scrape restricted sources, bypass gates or silently relabel machine-generated boxes as ground truth.
